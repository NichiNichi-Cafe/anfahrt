Anfahrt & Parken

Neue Parkplätze direkt beim NichiNichi.

Es gibt jetzt markierte NichiNichi-Parkplätze im Schatten – für zwei große oder drei normale Autos. Vom Parkplatz sind es nur etwa 30 Sekunden zu Fuß bis zum Café.

Directions & Parking

New parking spaces right by NichiNichi.

There are now marked NichiNichi parking spaces in the shade, with room for two large or three standard cars. The café is only about a 30-second walk away.

01

P1 + P2

P1 + P2

Please park space-efficiently so there is room for two large or three standard cars.

Keep the road clear

Please stay fully within the marked parking area and keep the road clear for traffic.

Please park as before near the entrance to Reinhardsau.

From the new parking area, the entrance to NichiNichi Café is just around the corner.
